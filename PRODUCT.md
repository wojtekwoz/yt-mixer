# PRODUCT.md

## Register

**Product.** Design serves the task. The whole surface is one play screen; there
is no marketing page.

## What it is

A DJ toy for YouTube. Two records spin side by side, one fat slider decides
which one you hear, and a queue holds what's next. Friends can join by link and
send in songs.

## Who uses it

A five-year-old, with a parent nearby. They cannot read fluently, cannot aim a
mouse precisely, and have never seen a mixer. They are on a laptop or tablet on
a kitchen table in the afternoon.

The parent is the secondary user: they open a session, share the link, and
approve requests. That work is real but it is not play, so it lives behind one
button.

## The one job

**Make the music you are hearing turn into a different song.** Everything on the
screen either does that, shows that, or feeds the queue that makes it possible.

## Design principles

1. **Subtraction is the method.** A control ships only if removing it would
   break the one job. When in doubt, cut it and let the device, the default, or
   the content do the work.
2. **Show, don't label.** State is carried by size, motion, and album art. A
   record that is spinning is playing. A record that is big is loud. No reading
   required.
3. **Spatial truth.** The slider sits directly under the two records and the
   knob is under whichever one you hear. Position means what it looks like it
   means.
4. **Every target is a toy target.** Nothing smaller than a fingertip, nothing
   revealed only on hover. Hover does not exist on a tablet.
5. **The content is the palette.** Chrome is white, near-black, and one accent.
   Colour comes from the cover art.

## Anti-references

- Pro DJ software (Serato, Traktor, rekordbox) — the register we are leaving.
- Primary-colour kids apps: red/blue/yellow, bubble gradients, rounded novelty
  typeface. The first reflex.
- Muted sage-and-blush "Montessori wooden toy" pastels. The second reflex.
- Cream / sand / parchment backgrounds.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, placeholders held to body contrast.
Everything keyboard reachable with a visible focus ring. Every animation has a
`prefers-reduced-motion` alternative, and playing state is never signalled by
motion alone.
